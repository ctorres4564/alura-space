- **[Iluminação]:** Luz natural suave de fim de tarde (golden hour) entrando pela janela com cortina translúcida, gerando contraste caloroso e sombras suaves.
- **[Lente/Equipamento]:** Lente prime cinematográfica 50mm f/1.8, sensor full frame, profundidade de campo rasa (bokeh suave no fundo).
- **Prompt Utilizado:** `A realistic cinematic 9:16 vertical shot in a cozy Brazilian living room during warm late afternoon golden hour. A Brazilian 4-year-old girl with curly brown hair, wearing a soft cream floral patterned blouse and pink pants, sits on a modern neutral sofa looking distressed and trying to communicate, gesturing slightly. In the background, slightly out of focus, her mother, a Brazilian woman in her 30s with dark hair tied back in a neat bun, wearing an olive-green button-up shirt and dark jeans, stands with her arms crossed looking skeptical and firm. Soft sunlight streaming through a sheer curtained window, indoor plants nearby, shallow depth of field, 50mm prime cinema lens look, photorealistic, emotive, natural film color grading.`
